Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Team Seven Marketing LLC ("we", "us") handles information collected through teamsevenmarketing.com (the "Site"). This Site is a company information site. Some of our marketing campaigns use separate websites, each with its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your name, email address and anything you include in your message.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit, for security and site operation.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, operate and secure the Site, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information collected through this Site.

Cookies

This Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Site and our business (such as hosting and email providers), when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer.

Data retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected and for our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

This Site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
