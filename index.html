Compliance

Quality traffic starts with clean practices.

Insurance and finance are regulated categories. We treat compliance as part of the product, not an afterthought.

  • Proper consent. Lead forms use clear TCPA consent language and a TrustedForm or Jornaya certificate on every lead. We never alter an advertiser's consent wording.
  • Honest advertising. No fake news layouts, fake testimonials, government-program angles or unsupported savings claims.
  • Platform policies. Campaigns follow Meta's financial products and services rules, with clear disclosures on every page.
  • Offer rules respected. Targeting is checked against each offer's allowed geos, states, devices and traffic types before launch.
